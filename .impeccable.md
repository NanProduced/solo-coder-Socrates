## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: General knowledge seekers, students, and professionals who need to digest complex information.
- **Context of Use**: "Just-in-time" reading assistance. Users are actively reading a webpage and need a companion to help them bridge the gap from "reading" to "understanding."
- **Goal**: Facilitate a "Aha!" moment where the user feels a spark of wisdom rather than just consuming a summary.

### Brand Personality
- **Voice**: Knowledgeable (博学的), Friendly (友善的), Clever (机灵的).
- **Tone**: The "Socrates" persona—modest but sharp, guiding rather than lecturing.
- **Anti-References**: No intrusive popups, no aggressive AI gradients/glows, no complex multi-step onboarding that interrupts the reading flow.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Style**: Minimalist, Notion-style. Clean lines, generous whitespace, and a focus on content.
- **Theme**: Adaptive/Dynamic. Must support Light/Dark modes to match the source webpage, ensuring visual continuity and maintaining the user's immersion.
- **Imagery**: Hand-drawn line art/sketches (Notion-style) if icons or illustrations are needed.
- **Color Palette**: Perceptually neutral base (Notion greys) with a single, clear accent color (Socrates Blue) for guidance.

### Design Principles
1. **Immersion First**: The assistant is a guest on the user's page. It should respect the visual context of the source content and never dominate the screen.
2. **Contextual Intelligence**: The "cleverness" should manifest in the *relevance* of the questions, not the complexity of the UI.
3. **Frictionless Interaction**: "即用即走" (Just-in-time). Minimizing the steps between clicking the extension and getting the first insightful question.
4. **Adaptive Harmony**: UI elements should feel like they belong to whatever environment they are in, especially regarding theme switching.
